I. The Moon Reflected in Water (Dōgen Zenji — Genjōkōan)

“The moon does not get wet, nor is the water broken. Even in a single dewdrop, the whole moon is reflected.”
Moonlight rests on a still mountain tarn beside Brú na Bóinne. Sweep a single closed circle (Ensō) across the parchment—or press O—to hold moon and water in one unbroken loop.
